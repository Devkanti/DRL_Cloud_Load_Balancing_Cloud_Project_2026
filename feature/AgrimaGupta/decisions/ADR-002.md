## 1. Purpose

This record documents the structured cost review of every AWS service originally specified in `PRD.md` ┬º12, carried out before Phase 4 work begins. The goal is to reduce unnecessary AWS expenditure without invalidating the DRL load-balancing research.

**Original estimated total:** $15ΓÇô35 USD  
**Revised estimated total:** $5ΓÇô15 USD  
**Research impact:** None ΓÇö all core research objectives O1ΓÇôO8 remain achievable.

## 8. Revised Cost Estimate

| Scenario | Original | Revised |
|----------|---------|---------|
| Conservative (4 hrs active/day, 14 exp days) | $12ΓÇô18 | **$4ΓÇô8** |
| Realistic (6 hrs active/day, 14 exp days) | $20ΓÇô30 | **$7ΓÇô12** |
| Maximum (8 hrs active/day, 14 exp days) | $35ΓÇô45 | **$10ΓÇô15** |

**Primary cost drivers remaining:**
1. **ALB** ΓÇö $0.0225/hr fixed + LCU charges during active experiments (~$1.30 fixed + ~$0.50 LCU estimated)
2. **EC2** ΓÇö 5 t2.micro during experiment sessions (~$2ΓÇô5 depending on hours used)

> This design aims to minimise AWS expenditure and stay within available Free Tier and student credits where possible. It does not guarantee a zero-cost experiment campaign. ALB and EC2 will incur charges during active experiment windows.
